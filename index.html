FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How does laundry.online work?

Place your order online or on a quick call. Our rider comes to your doorstep for pickup, your clothes are processed at our facility, and delivered back to you — usually within 48–72 hours.

How many days does processing take?

Standard wash & fold / wash & iron takes 48–72 hours. Express orders are delivered within 24 hours.

Is pickup and delivery free?

Yes. We offer completely free doorstep pickup and delivery on every order, with no minimum order value.

Can you deliver in 24 hours?

Yes — choose Express Laundry at checkout and your clothes will be back within 24 hours.

How are clothes measured / weighed?

Clothes are weighed on an electronic scale in front of you at pickup, and you receive a digital receipt instantly.

What if something gets damaged or lost?

Every order is covered by our quality & care promise. We inspect every garment and look after any claim fairly and quickly.
