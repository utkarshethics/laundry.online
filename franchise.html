FAQs

Questions partners ask us first

What is the total investment to start a laundry.online outlet?

₹1,00,000 plus GST. That is the whole number: ₹25,000 franchise fee, ₹15,000 branding and signage, ₹20,000 launch and marketing, and ₹40,000 of working capital for the first two months. Machines are not in that figure — see the next question.

Are the machines really free, or are they on rent?

Genuinely free, and they stay with you. We install the commercial washing, drying and ironing equipment at your outlet and it is yours to use for as long as you run the franchise — no rent, no lease, no monthly deduction from your order revenue, no lock-in. Chemicals and packaging come from us too, at partner rates. This is the single biggest difference between our model and a conventional laundry franchise, which asks you to buy ₹4–9 lakh of equipment on day one.

How much can I actually earn?

At 300 orders a month on a ₹220 average ticket, the outlet keeps roughly ₹23,000 a month after chemicals, riders, rent and staff — with no equipment cost at all, since we supply the machinery free. Break-even is about 157 orders a month. These are projections, not guarantees, and the first two to three months are usually thin while you build your customer base. The full arithmetic is on this page.

Do I need previous laundry or business experience?

No. Around 70% of our partners had never run a laundry before. The 7-day training covers machine operation, fabric and stain handling, pricing, the booking app and customer handling.

Will another partner open near me and compete?

Territories are allocated on a pin-code basis. We map your catchment against live and planned outlets before signing, and you get written exclusivity for the codes you operate.

What support do I get after launch?

A named relationship manager, the booking and CRM dashboard, shared rider capacity in your area, launch marketing, monthly performance reviews and a 24x7 operations helpline. Chemicals and packaging are supplied to you at partner rates.

How long does it take to open?

Typically 21 days from signing the agreement. Two to three weeks of that is equipment installation and training, both of which we schedule around you.

Can I run more than one outlet later?

Yes. Partners who hit steady volumes in their first catchment frequently open a second pin-code cluster. Existing partners get first refusal on adjacent territories before they are offered to new applicants.
