1. Transparent Pricing Philosophy

At laundry.online, we believe in honest, predictable rates with zero hidden convenience fees, zero fuel surcharges, and zero doorstep markups. This document outlines our standard rate card, billing mechanisms, and fee adjustments.

2. The ₹50 Advance Slot Reservation

The ₹50 advance payment secures an expert logistics rider visit to your doorstep. This fee is 100% credited against your final laundry invoice. If you cancel up to 2 hours before your scheduled time slot, the ₹50 fee is fully refunded to your original payment method.

4. Invoicing & Final Pricing

All prices quoted on the website and mobile platforms are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are the final amount payable – no GST is added. Official order receipts are issued by STAGE-X upon order completion.

Payments on this website and for all associated doorstep invoices are processed directly by STAGE-X via Razorpay. Tailors and delivery riders are salaried or vendor-partnered by STAGE-X; please do not pay riders directly in cash unless specifically indicated on the invoice.
